SlotCenterToCenter2D

SlotCenterToCenter2D(p0, p1, width)

Create a straight Slot from the centres of its two round ends.

Parameters

ArgumentMeaning
p0Centre of the first round end as (x, y) in sketch coordinates, in mm.
p1Centre of the second round end as (x, y) in sketch coordinates, in mm.
widthTotal Slot width in mm. This is also the diameter of each round end.

Example

SlotCenterToCenter2D((-10, 0), (10, 0), 6)

Current limits

p0 and p1 must be different points. The straight part runs between these two end centres.

SlotOverall2D

SlotOverall2D(p0, p1, width)

Create a straight Slot from its two outermost end points.

Parameters

ArgumentMeaning
p0Outermost point of the first round end as (x, y) in sketch coordinates, in mm.
p1Outermost point of the second round end as (x, y) in sketch coordinates, in mm.
widthTotal Slot width in mm. This is also the diameter of each round end.

Example

SlotOverall2D((-15, 0), (15, 0), 6)

Current limits

The distance from p0 to p1 is the total Slot length, including both round ends, and must be larger than width.

SlotCenterPoint2D

SlotCenterPoint2D(center, end, width)

Create a straight Slot from its centre and one round-end centre.

Parameters

ArgumentMeaning
centerCentre of the complete Slot as (x, y) in sketch coordinates, in mm.
endCentre of one round end as (x, y) in sketch coordinates, in mm.
widthTotal Slot width in mm. This is also the diameter of each round end.

Example

SlotCenterPoint2D((0, 0), (10, 0), 6)

Current limits

center and end must differ. The other round-end centre is mirrored through center.

SlotArcThreePoint2D

SlotArcThreePoint2D(start, through, end, width)

Create a curved Slot whose centre arc passes through three points.

Parameters

ArgumentMeaning
startStart point of the Slot centre arc as (x, y) in sketch coordinates, in mm.
throughA point that the centre arc must pass through, as (x, y) in mm.
endEnd point of the Slot centre arc as (x, y) in sketch coordinates, in mm.
widthTotal Slot width in mm, measured perpendicular to the centre arc.

Example

SlotArcThreePoint2D((-15, 0), (0, 10), (15, 0), 6)

Current limits

start, through and end must be three different points.

SlotArcCenter2D

SlotArcCenter2D(center, start, width, *, end=None, sweep=None)

Create a curved Slot from an arc centre and start point.

Parameters

ArgumentMeaning
centerCentre of the underlying circular arc as (x, y) in sketch coordinates, in mm.
startStart point of the Slot centre arc as (x, y) in sketch coordinates, in mm.
widthTotal Slot width in mm, measured perpendicular to the centre arc.
endOptional end point of the centre arc as (x, y) in mm.
sweepOptional positive sweep angle in degrees.

Example

SlotArcCenter2D((0, 0), (12, 0), 6, sweep=120)

Current limits

Supply exactly one of end= or sweep=. center and start must differ. sweep must be greater than 0 and less than 360 degrees.
