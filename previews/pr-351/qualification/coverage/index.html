SolidCraft functionality coverage

This page separates two questions. First: how much of the useful SolidCraft capability surface is exposed coherently through Forge? Second: how much of that Forge support has been functionally qualified against native SolidCraft?

Coverage is capability-based, not command-count based. One row represents a meaningful CAD capability such as primitives, extrusion, fillet/chamfer, components or joints. SolidCraft may expose several commands and options behind one row.
