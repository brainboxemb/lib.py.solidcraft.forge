Native whole-sheet perforation

Manufacturing perforations stay native sheet and flat-pattern history

Verified scpf 60 × 40 × 2 mm sheet; Ø5 mm perforations; 10 mm pitch; 5 mm margin; square pattern

What this verifies

A 60 × 40 × 2 mm native sheet carries a parameterized manufacturing perforation field. The display B-rep stays uncut; flat-pattern/DXF output and measured volume carry the native perforation semantics.

scpf/model.py
"""Public Verification model for SolidCraft whole-sheet perforations."""


class SheetPerforate:
    width = 60.0
    depth = 40.0
    thickness = 2.0
    bend_radius = 2.0
    k_factor = 0.5
    perforation_diameter = 5.0
    perforation_pitch = 10.0
    perforation_margin = 5.0

    def build(self):
        with Sketch(name="Blank", plane="XY") as blank:
            Rectangle2D(self.width, self.depth)

        sheet = blank.sheet_metal(
            thickness=self.thickness,
            bend_radius=self.bend_radius,
            k_factor=self.k_factor,
            rule_name="Perforation Rule",
        )
        return sheet.perforate(
            diameter=self.perforation_diameter,
            pitch=self.perforation_pitch,
            margin=self.perforation_margin,
            pattern="square",
        )


SheetPerforate()

Engineering explanation

This verification covers a native manufacturing perforation field, not ordinary B-rep hole cuts.

SolidCraft’s sheet.perforate contract intentionally keeps the display B-rep as one sheet while carrying perforations in flat-pattern, DXF and measured-volume semantics. The ordinary 3D preview may therefore look like a solid plate and is not the visual proof for this capability.

The native checks prove the saved design contains one PerforateFeature and that sheet.flat_pattern reports 15 square-pattern perforations at Ø5 mm, 10 mm pitch and 5 mm margin.

The manufacturing-flat-pattern visual check exports the saved .sc through native sheet.export_dxf. Actual is rendered from DXF PERFORATION circles; Goal is the approved 5 × 3 field. The first Ø5 centre is at (-22.5, -12.5): that leaves the declared 5 mm material clearance from the sheet edge to the hole contour, after which centres repeat on the 10 mm square pitch. The check fails if native manufacturing output loses holes, moves them, changes their diameter or becomes a solid blank.

Forge deliberately does not replace this feature with repeated solid holes.

  2. Apply one native whole-sheet perforation field
  3. Mutate diameter, pitch and margin and inspect the rebuilt flat pattern
